# Lattice Poly Synth
A disciplined Ink & Signal-derived voice panel for a polyphonic synth. The interface uses a dark architectural shell, low raised panels, and a single teal signal accent to separate peer synthesis regions without visual noise. OSC, FILTER, ENV, and MOTION are equal citizens arranged as a lattice: tone generation and shaping on the left, articulation and movement on the right. Typography separates the compact brand header, section labels, control labels, and value readouts through scale, weight, and muted color rather than extra decoration.
