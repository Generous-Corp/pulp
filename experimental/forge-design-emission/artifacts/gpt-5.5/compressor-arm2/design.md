# Atlas Bus Compressor
A controlled Ink & Signal panel language for a modern stereo bus compressor: dark machined surfaces, disciplined teal signal emphasis, restrained top-left depth, and typography that separates brand, section labels, control labels, and numeric readouts. Threshold is the tactile center of gravity; related timing, blend, and level controls orbit it in subordinate groups.
