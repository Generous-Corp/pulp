# Vector Utility
A clinical utility-grid language for compact mastering controls. Dark ink surfaces, restrained raised panels, and one teal signal accent establish hierarchy without a hero. Typography separates brand, section labels, control labels, and numeric readouts with Inter for interface text and JetBrains Mono for values. Negative space and vertical section rhythm make input, vector shaping, and output relationships fast to scan.
