# Lattice
A calm, dense voice panel language for polyphonic synths. Dark neutral surfaces sit on a slightly deeper base; each section is a raised card with a hairline border and a small accent dot marking its header. Jost carries the product title; Inter carries all labels in small tracked caps. Color is reserved: accent teal marks section identity, primary blue is left to control chrome. Sections are peers — equal width, equal rhythm, no hero.
