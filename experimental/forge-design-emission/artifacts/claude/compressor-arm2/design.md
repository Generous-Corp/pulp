# Atlas Bus Compressor
Ink & Signal, weighted for authority. One material idea: matte graphite panels
over near-black ink, controls turned from a single dark-metal gradient, light
from the top-left. One accent: signal teal, spent only where the instrument
speaks — the hero ring and its readout. Depth is restrained: a recessed well
seats the hero, raised satellite panels sit one step above the surface.
Type roles: Jost for brand and section voices (tracked uppercase), Inter for
control labels, JetBrains Mono for every numeric readout. Hierarchy is
topological before it is decorative: THRESHOLD is the center of gravity at
roughly twice satellite scale; DYNAMICS (ratio, attack, release) and OUTPUT
(makeup, mix) follow the signal flow left to right.
