# Vector Utility

A clinical mastering utility on the Ink & Signal material: near-black ink surfaces, one raised card material for signal sections, and a single teal signal accent used only to mark structure (brand tick, section bars). No hero — five identical 56px controls sit in three signal-flow sections (INPUT, STEREO IMAGE, OUTPUT) so the eye scans left to right along the bus. Jost carries brand and section voice, Inter carries control labels, JetBrains Mono carries readouts. Depth is restrained to one soft top-left-lit drop per card.
