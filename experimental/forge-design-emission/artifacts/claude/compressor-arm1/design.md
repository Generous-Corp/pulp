# Atlas

Atlas is an authoritative stereo bus compressor faceplate in a modern analog idiom: a charcoal gradient panel, hairline dividers, all-caps Jost labels with wide tracking, and JetBrains Mono readouts. Warm amber is reserved for the hero. THRESHOLD is the unmistakable center of gravity — one oversized knob on a raised plate, its readout set in the primary amber. RATIO, ATTACK, and RELEASE form a subordinate trio to the right; MAKEUP and MIX sit below them at reduced visual weight. Nothing competes with the hero: no second accent, no decorative flourishes, generous negative space around the threshold plate.
