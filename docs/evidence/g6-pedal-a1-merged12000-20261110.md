## Contract and limits

This profile uses the existing `MpeVoiceAllocator` and MPE generation owner.
Member-channel pitch, pressure, and CC74 affect the matching live generation's
audio; manager-channel expression is typed `UnsupportedExpression`. Physical
NoteOff under CC64 retains one bounded generation-scoped hold and dispatches
release through the allocator when the last applicable member/zone pedal rises.
CC120 hard-silences only the addressed member or zone; CC121 clears its hold
and controllers; CC123 sends scoped physical NoteOff. A steal hard-resets its
victim and has no separate tail queue. The A1 output law is fixed
`1/sqrt(8)` gain, ordered eight-slot stereo sum, four in-slot linear release
frames, and zero output after completion for the scalar fixture.

adapters and hosted AU/CLAP/VST3 behavior, Linux and Windows execution,

saved-bank activation/edit/undo, arbitrary authored graph/polyphony shapes,

matrix, RTSan graph run, callback allocation/lock/wait/I/O audit, WCET, or
