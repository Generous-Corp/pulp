RTSan receipt (2026-11-13): [`g12-rtsan-receipt-20261113.md`](../evidence/g12-rtsan-receipt-20261113.md)
shows the G12 prepared gain activation fixture is RTSan-clean on both serial
backends (routed selection asserted: `blocks_processed == kCallbacks`, direct
`== 0`, no walk fallbacks), and that the analyzer catches planted malloc,
mutex, sleep and write calls inside the same annotated boundary. It does not
cover Linux or LLVM 20, other sample-rate/block geometries, parallel execution,
other fixtures, or timing/deadline qualification; it is one N=1 run per case on
a loaded shared macOS arm64 host, built with Homebrew LLVM 21. The hosted
`rtsan` workflow job remains dispatch-only.

Jitter bar reading: the closeout requirement "p99 jitter below 10% of one
frame" (`docs/reports/g12-closeout-20261106.md`) is read as 0.1 sample period,
where a frame is one sample per channel (about 2.08 us at 48 kHz, 2.27 us at
44.1 kHz, 1.04 us at 96 kHz), not 10% of a callback block. No receipt has met
or measured against that bar yet.

fixtures (`tools/graph_paced_timing_matrix.py`) is tooling only; its usage,
receipt schema, limits and the quiet-host command are in
`docs/evidence/paced-timing-matrix-tooling-20261113.md`.
